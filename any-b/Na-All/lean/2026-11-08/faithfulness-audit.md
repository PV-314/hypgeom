## Checks and limits

I matched the two attached 08a files to the ZIP members by SHA-256. I also compared the released local Lean sources and three Python evidence checkers to the copies inspected, checked the ZIP CRC, and reran all three evidence checkers using inputs matched byte-for-byte to the archive. They reproduced the 43 of 192 tuples below (4.4), their inclusion in the 175, the 162 equations, the 94 recorded solution lists, the 84/6/4 distribution, both exceptional sequences, and the 205 coefficient checks. The additional cutoff audit above is independent of those three checkers.

The reviewed ZIP has SHA-256 `1e9e521c1a40e39ca1386ed9478a9a1a57740b9864fd39ef107d26ef0fc00645`. The build and the 542-declaration axiom audit are the release’s recorded results. This audit was a mathematical source comparison and finite arithmetic replay. It did not rerun the full Java enumeration, the 94 PARI descents, or the Lean build. The external results cited by the manuscript and the Lean premises identified above remain outside this audit’s independent proofs.

Two historical issues sometimes listed with this paper are already resolved in the supplied texts. For Lemma 2.1, 08a lines 643–667 explicitly exclude the zero pair and give the cross-product test, and the cited revised JNT proof has the successor-determinant factor. For Lemma 3.5(a), 08a lines 990–1001 and 1209–1218 use the strict final bounds, which survive the weak intermediate comparison in revised JNT Lemma 3.9(a). Neither requires another correction to 08a. The §5.6 distinction between 175 tuples and 162 equations and the requirement that five squares exist before invoking (4.4) are also present and checked.

No manuscript, proof module, dependency or release file was edited in this audit.
